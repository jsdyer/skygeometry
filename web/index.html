Understand the frame

J2000 inputs are rotated for precession and nutation at the observation time. “Of date” inputs already use that date’s equator. This is fixed-direction geometry: stellar proper motion, parallax, aberration and moving-object ephemerides are not applied.

Read the horizon

Altitude is 0° at a level horizon and 90° overhead. Azimuth runs clockwise from north: east 90°, south 180°, west 270°. Buildings, terrain, clouds and light pollution are not modeled. Refraction is an approximate standard-atmosphere correction.

Your observing notes stay yours

Coordinates and CSVs stay in this browser tab. Location is saved only when you choose “Remember location.” Location permission is optional. The shared CoolCode counter receives aggregate page opens, not your location or targets.
